# Sentinel — Demo Script (90 seconds)

## Pre-demo setup
- Open https://sentinel-gold-xi.vercel.app in a fresh browser tab
- Ensure the page is loaded and showing the landing/hero section
- Have the browser at 1440px width (desktop)

## Script

**[0:00 — 0:10] Landing page**

> "This is Sentinel — a Mind that watches your community and catches conflicts before they escalate. It remembers interaction patterns over weeks, matches them to prior trajectories, and drafts interventions autonomously."

*Point to the headline and the 3-step "how it works" section.*

**[0:10 — 0:20] Enter the dashboard**

*Click "Try the demo". The page scrolls down to the dashboard.*

> "This is the Pixel Forge community — a Discord-style pixel art creator community. On the left is the community feed. On the right is the Conflict Watch panel."

*Point to the feed and the Conflict Watch panel.*

**[0:20 — 0:35] The active conflict**

> "Sentinel has flagged an active conflict between Alex and Jordan — two community regulars who've been arguing about palette philosophy. The trajectory shows it escalated from neutral to frustrated to hostile over 12 days."

*Point to the red 'Active' conflict card in the right panel.*

**[0:35 — 0:55] Click the conflict — Pattern Timeline**

*Click the Alex vs Jordan conflict card. The detail modal opens on the Pattern Timeline tab.*

> "The Mind's assessment is at the top — it matched this conflict to a prior pattern: Chris vs Sam, which escalated to a ban. Below that, the interaction timeline shows each exchange, color-coded by tone. You can see the shift from a normal creative disagreement to personal attacks."

*Point to the blue 'Mind's Assessment' box, then scroll through the interaction cards.*

**[0:55 — 0:75] Mind Sessions tab**

*Click the 'Mind Sessions' tab.*

> "This is the persistence proof. Four real monitoring runs from the Mind's conversation history — not seeded data. Session 1: 'no conflict detected.' Session 2: 'I remember the Aug 14 exchange — the argument has moved off palettes and onto each other.' Session 3: 'the pattern is forming fast enough.' Session 4: 'someone should intervene — the window has tightened from this week to the next few days.'"

*Point to the LIVE badge and scroll through the sessions.*

**[0:75 — 0:90] Intervention tab**

*Click the 'Intervention' tab.*

> "And here's the autonomous action — the Mind drafted a full de-escalation message, ready to post with one click. It named the dynamic, addressed both members specifically, suggested a reset period, and even included meta-notes on why it structured the message that way. This is a Mind that doesn't just detect conflicts — it remembers them, reasons about them, and acts on them."

*Point to the drafted message and the 'Send to #feedback' button.*

## Key points to emphasize

1. **The Mind is real** — the session log shows live output from the Minds API, not seeded data
2. **Persistence is real** — the Mind genuinely remembers across sessions (it referenced the Aug 14 exchange in session 2)
3. **The Mind has personality** — it pushed back on being told what to say, refused to use templates, and gave honest assessments
4. **The intervention is autonomous** — the Mind drafted it without being given a template, and included meta-reasoning about its own draft
5. **The pattern match is the money shot** — "this trajectory ended in a ban last time, and we're on the same path"

## What to avoid saying

- Don't say "simulated" or "mocked" without context — the Mind's outputs are real, only the community data is seeded
- Don't overhype — let the Mind's own words do the work
- Don't skip the LIVE badge — it's the proof that this isn't seeded
